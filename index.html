Methods

One protein, three layers of evidence.

The display keeps abundance, site composition, and comparison confidence distinct so that each visual claim remains tied to the layer that supports it.

Protein abundance

Age trajectories

Median-normalized scaled intensity is summarized across six biological replicates at each age. The refreshed July 30 normalized values are used directly: upstream normalization resolved the prior group/run structure, so no secondary run adjustment is applied.

Site composition

PSM-derived percentages

Glycan composition is calculated at each observed N-glycosite and can be viewed as broad classes, exact structures, or Shannon diversity across three age bins or all age points.

Cross-organ comparison

Matched sites only

A site is high confidence when total PSM is at least 5 in each organ being compared. Lower-confidence observations stay visible with an asterisk but never enter pairwise distances.

Age bins

Young: 1, 3, 6 months · Middle-aged: 9, 12, 16 months · Old: 19, 22, 28 months.

Pairwise distance

Total variation measures the absolute redistribution of glycan-class composition between matched organ-site profiles.

Missingness

Absent measurements are reported as not detected. They are not silently imputed or converted to zero abundance.

Learning

A field guide to reading the atlas.

Use these definitions as a quick reference while moving between a protein trajectory and its site-specific glycan evidence.

Glycoprotein

A protein carrying one or more covalently attached glycans. Total-protein abundance and glycan composition answer related but different questions.

Glycosite

A specific amino-acid position where a glycan was observed. Atlas labels such as N323 identify an asparagine-linked site at residue 323.

Glycan class

A broad structural category—such as high mannose, fucosylated, or sialylated—used to make complex glycan inventories comparable.

Exact structure

The measured monosaccharide composition for a glycan assignment. Several exact structures can contribute to one broad glycan class.

Shannon diversity

A summary of how many glycan classes are present and how evenly abundance is distributed among them. Higher values indicate a more diverse composition.

PSM confidence

Peptide-spectrum matches support a site observation. The atlas marks total PSM below 5 as low confidence and excludes it from cross-organ comparisons.

Methods

One protein, three layers of evidence.

The display keeps abundance, site composition, and comparison confidence distinct so that each visual claim remains tied to the layer that supports it.

Protein abundance

Age trajectories

Globally median-normalized scaled intensity is summarized as mean ± SEM across detected biological replicates. Datasets with only one or two detected replicates are marked with an asterisk.

Site composition

Two distinct summaries

Overview bars, hover values, and dominant type pool all PSMs. Age trajectories show the mean of sample percentages ± SEM. Neither treats missing samples as zero.

Heterogeneity

Sample and pooled views

Weighted glycan types and Shannon H can be shown as sample means ± SEM or as a PSM-pooled value per age group. Automatic mode uses sample means for evidence tier 4 or higher and pooled values otherwise.

Age bins

Atlas Y: 1, 3, 6 months · MA: 9, 12, 16 months · O: 19, 22, 28 months. Myelin groupings follow the source dataset.

Pairwise distance

Total variation measures the absolute redistribution of glycan-class composition between matched organ-site profiles.

Missingness

Absent measurements are reported as not detected. They are not silently imputed or converted to zero abundance.

Learning

A field guide to reading the atlas.

Use these definitions as a quick reference while moving between a protein trajectory and its site-specific glycan evidence.

Glycoprotein

A protein carrying one or more covalently attached glycans. Total-protein abundance and glycan composition answer related but different questions.

Glycosite

A specific amino-acid position where a glycan was observed. Atlas labels such as N323 identify an asparagine-linked site at residue 323.

Glycan class

A broad structural category—such as high mannose, fucosylated, or sialylated—used to make complex glycan inventories comparable.

Exact structure

The measured monosaccharide composition for a glycan assignment. Several exact structures can contribute to one broad glycan class.

Shannon diversity

A measure of both the number and evenness of glycan types. Weighted glycan types (effective types) is exp(Shannon H), expressed as a more intuitive count.

PSM confidence

Peptide-spectrum matches support a site observation. An asterisk marks fewer than 5 site PSMs; seven evidence tiers are shown in the explorer.

Raw data

Versioned inputs, reproducible outputs.

The E330 website data release is generated reproducibly from the September E322 glycoproteomics outputs and seven normalized proteomics datasets. The browser reads processed JSON; raw experiment files are not served.

Proteomics

Seven normalized datasets

Five atlas organs plus male and female myelin. Data are summarized by dataset and age, with detected replicate counts.

Glycoproteomics

Seven processed datasets

Site records include PSM-pooled class percentages, exact glycan compositions, Shannon H, weighted glycan types, and evidence tiers.
